- ParallaxComponent: adapted from the user-supplied GSAP/Lenis component credited to [Osmo](https://www.osmo.supply/). The original image layers are replaced with a vector interpretation of the user-supplied event poster keyhole; animation and ticker cleanup are scoped to the component.

- Fraunces, Big Shoulders Display, and Spline Sans Mono are bundled through Fontsource. Font license files are in `public/licenses/`.

- ShutterText and Input: adapted from the user-supplied component snippets. Shutter slices retain the supplied timing; colors match the event theme and reduced-motion is supported. Input uses explicit controlled clearing and accessible icon-button labels.

- PromptInputBox: text-only adaptation of the user-supplied AI prompt box, with its rounded container, auto-growing textarea, and circular send button. Integrates the existing challenge API; unsupported media and tool toggles are omitted.
